Universal Health Aid Columbus Sponsors

We seek to form longterm community partnerships to further the success and sustainability of our health care initiatives.

By partnering with other compassionate, driven organizations and individuals, we hope to create an even greater impact in bettering the community. Each and every one of our partners helps us provide the highest quality experience to the Greater Columbus Area. Our community partners truly empower us to fulfill our mission.

Interested in Sponsoring UHA Columbus?

Universal Health Aid is an innovative and progressive non-profit organization dedicated to improving universal health through preventive medicine, health education, and leadership development. We are student founded and run with the help of our volunteers and sponsors. In each of our chapters, we hold free medical screenings for under/uninsured residents, providing high quality services and physicians of multiple specialties.

We are always looking for sponsors and would genuinely appreciate your continued support in accomplishing our screening. Thank you to our generous sponsors who have supported us greatly in the past to help get us to where we are today. Your continued support is deeply appreciated.

Please contact our VP of Finance, Shreyaa Mani, at shreyaa.mani@universal-health-aid.org if you would like to speak with someone about sponsoring our organization.
