Annual Health Screening

Hilltop Community Worship Center

What we provide

Medical Resources

Physicians and health professionals from the Columbus community provide immediate medical attention for all patients. Our screenings include the following specialities:

  • Family and Internal Medicine Physicians
  • Pediatrics
  • Nurses
  • Mental Health Counselors
  • Ohio State Dentistry and Optometry students

Non-Profit Connections

Many Columbus residents do not know about the large variety of public health organizations around them. These organizations work with many different issues and are always eager to serve those in need. We help connect communities with non-profits that will provide vital resources throughout the year.

Health Education

An important key to decreasing health care barriers in our community is through improving health literacy. Many of our visiting non-profits spread their message through awareness and education. Our screening will provide education on a wide range of issues important to our community. We hope to give patients the knowledge to make independent choices involving their own health care.

Immediate Resources

We provide food, education packets, and health kits to provide holistic and comprehensive care for our community: healthcare kits (toothbrush/toothpaste, floss, condoms, tampons, etc.), healthy snacks and a meal through the Hilltop Community Worship Center, and health education brochures on topics such as mental health or the opioid epidemic.

A look back

Weeks of Service

This is how one of our screenings came together: four Saturdays at the Hilltop Community Worship Center in February 2020.

February 8th · 1:30PM – 4PM

Drug Prevention and Mental Health Services

The Franklin County Public Health Department and Safe Point came to connect residents with naloxone training and drug prevention and disposal programs. Additionally, we hosted the Franklin County Suicide Prevention Coalition to help connect families with mental health education and suicide prevention resources. Finally, Ohio State Respiratory Therapy students provided education based assistance on a wide variety of diseases and conditions related to the lungs and resources for those suffering with nicotine addiction.

February 15th · 1:30PM – 4PM

Infant Mortality, Food Insecurity, and Women’s Health Services

We hosted Moms2B and Planned Parenthood to help connect residents with safe sex resources and prenatal education. Additionally, James Cancer Hospital provided information for how women can attain mammograms and pap smears. Finally, Columbus Public Health provided a general screening (blood pressure, heart rate, and more) and resources educating on maintaining a healthy daily lifestyle.

February 22nd · 11:30AM – 4PM

Medical Screening

Our comprehensive medical screening this day had many health professionals: Internal Medicine, Family Medicine, Pediatrics, and Nurse Practitioners to provide immediate assistance and check-ups on all patients. Additionally, we had multiple counselors available for residents to speak with to discuss mental health-related issues. We also provided free health care kits with many basic necessities (toiletries, tampons, condoms, etc).

February 29th · 1:30PM – 4PM

Insurance Information and Feedback

On our last screening day, we hosted the Legal Aid Society of Columbus to answer issues/questions related to health care paperwork (such as Medicare/Medicaid). Additionally, ENCompass helped link residents with local public health resources that meet their needs. Ohio State Dental Students and KidSMILES came to provide general screenings, health care items, and education for childhood hygiene. Finally, we also hosted Prevent Blindness to help provide immediate assistance, screenings, and referrals.

Attending Organizations

ENCompass

Founded at Ohio State University, ENCompass seeks to bridge the gap between medical and social care. At a variety of hospitals, primary care centers and food pantries, ENCompass connects clients with close-by public health resources that meet their needs.

Learn More

Franklin County Suicide Prevention Coalition

Franklin County Suicide Prevention Coalition (FCSPC) is a coalition dedicated to increase suicide prevention efforts in our community. With their trainings, speakers, and resources, FCSPC works to increase awareness and decrease stigma. FCSPC also connects those suffering with immediate resources, such as screenings or crisis lines.

Learn More

KidSMILES

KidSMILES clinic is an organization dedicated to improving childhood dental care in Columbus. At their facility, they provide dental services at a discounted and affordable price. Additionally, clinic members are actively involved in community outreach and events to educate children on proper dental hygiene.

Learn More

Legal Aid Society of Columbus

The Legal Aid Society of Columbus (LASC) is an organization that provides legal aid to impoverished and disadvantaged people. LASC advocates, educates, and empowers residents on a number of complicated issues such as rent, health care, wage theft, and more.

Learn More

Moms2B

Created in 2010, Moms2B has been fighting high infant mortality rates in Columbus. Every week, Moms2B provides women with education on many topics, a supportive environment, on-site child care, and a healthy meal.

Learn More

Planned Parenthood

Planned Parenthood is a nationwide leader in sexual education and health services. Their clinics provide essential resources such as STD testing and treatment, emergency contraception, and pregnancy services. Planned Parenthood also fights to end misinformation and stigma around sexual and reproductive health through their wide variety of patient education and health resources.

Learn More

Prevent Blindness

Prevent Blindness is dedicated to spreading the gift of sight to children from low economic backgrounds through education, awareness, and advocacy. Across the country, Prevent Blindness helps link children with free eye exams and discounted glasses.

Learn More

Safe Point

Safe Point is an innovative program run under Equitas Health that addresses community drug use. Participants can receive many resources, such as STD/HIV/Hepatitis C testing, housing and legal assistance, naloxone training, and more. They are known for their syringe exchange program to help prevent the spread of infectious diseases.

Learn More
