Application Process

Prospective applicants must complete the following:

  • A Written Application
  • Attend an information session

Afterwards, selected applicants will interview with members from our Executive Board. Individuals who show the most initiative, vision, and passion will be chosen to join our newest class of UHA Columbus!

For any questions, including this semester’s application and information session dates, please contact our President, Lasya Kopuri, at lasya.kopuri@universal-health-aid.org.
